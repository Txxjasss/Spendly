Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the information needed to run the service:

  • Account information — your name, email address and a securely hashed password when you register.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic technical data — such as browser type and session cookies needed to keep you signed in.

We do not collect bank account details, card numbers or connect to your financial institutions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Store, display and summarise your expenses.
  • Keep the service secure and prevent misuse.
  • Improve Spendly's features and reliability.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database and is accessible only to you through your account. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account at any time, after which your personal data and expense records will be removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party providers for things like hosting and web fonts. These providers may process limited technical information (such as your IP address) only as needed to deliver their service. We do not share your expense data with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Your Rights

You can view, update or delete the information in your account at any time. If you would like a copy of your data or have it removed entirely, please contact us.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the Spendly team.
